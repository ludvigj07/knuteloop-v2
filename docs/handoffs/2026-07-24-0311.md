## Gotchas / things to remember

- The 2027 goal is 100+ schools, not a 5–10-school pilot. Landing fewer is useful evidence, but the rollout should not be artificially capped.
- School payment is an adoption tool; regional category-exclusive sponsorship is the proposed long-term engine. Sponsor pricing remains unvalidated, while the pilot usage/payment and ice-purchase proxy are real data.
- Knuteloop's FOMO is organic and comes from real school activity. Do not manufacture engagement with streaks, sound, confetti, or celebration layers.
- V1 is product archaeology, not code to port blindly. Preserve the useful home-page jobs while removing flashy/gamified clutter.
- The official school Instagram post is the proven launch mechanism. Knuteloop itself, not Ludvig's personal founder identity, should be the student-facing brand.
