The intervals vary the database allowance only. They are not confidence intervals and do not cover all possible media usage or price changes. An indicative contingency budget excluding VAT is NOK 9,000 / 16,000 / 35,000. Multiplying these budgets by 1.25 gives NOK 11,250 / 20,000 / 43,750 as an illustrative cash reserve if 25% VAT must also be funded. Actual VAT treatment and deductibility were not determined.

### Usage assumptions and reproducible arithmetic

- 30 days of activity, with all modeled traffic occurring within this month. No division of the season traffic over multiple months.
- 100 **billable media downloads** per monthly active user per calendar day, averaged over the whole user cohort. No savings are credited for on-device caching. This is an assumption, not measured v2 usage. A person opening the app less often may view more items per visit.
- 12 media submissions per user over the month, retaining the older model's upload assumption. The old report records about 15 submissions per user in the v1 pilot. Text-only submissions reduce storage; they are not credited here.
- Photo-only scenario: 1 MB delivered per photo. In the mixed scenario, 60% of uploads and views are photos and 40% are video; actual upload and view mixes can differ.
- Mixed scenario: 8 MB delivered per video viewing on average, including partial plays. Stored video is assumed to occupy 27 MB including derived qualities, based on the older model's illustrative 15 MB source and 1.8 multiplier. No video pipeline measurements exist yet.
- Two EU storage copies are budgeted at USD 0.02/GB-month total. For simplicity, the whole ending media inventory is charged for the entire month; gradual uploads would reduce this small line. Actual replication/storage settings remain to be confirmed.
- Photos use Bunny Standard European delivery at USD 0.01/GB. Videos assume Bunny Stream **Volume** delivery at USD 0.005/GB. This price depends on selecting that delivery tier. Standard video delivery can cost more.
- Decimal units: 1 GB = 1,000 MB; 1 TB = 1,000 GB.
- USD/NOK = **9.2957**, EUR/NOK = **10.8035**, Norges Bank September 4, 2026 rates, retrieved directly through its public CSV API. These are indicative mid-market rates; billing FX/card spread and future rates may differ.

For N users, mixed-media cost in NOK is:

```text
photo delivery GB = N × 30 × 100 × 0.6 × 1 / 1000
video delivery GB = N × 30 × 100 × 0.4 × 8 / 1000
ending stored GB = N × 12 × (0.6 × 1 + 0.4 × 27) / 1000
media NOK = (photo GB × 0.01 + video GB × 0.005 + stored GB × 0.02) × 9.2957
total NOK = media NOK + server allowance + database allowance + 3750 + operations allowance
```

At 20,000 users: 36,000 GB photo delivery + 192,000 GB video delivery + 2,736 GB stored. Costs are respectively NOK 3,346.45 + 8,923.87 + 508.66 = **12,778.98** media total. The photo-only alternative is 60,000 GB delivery + 240 GB stored = **5,622.04**.

### Sensitivity, one-time costs and exclusions

- **Three times the scrolling** (300 downloads/user/day at the same sizes/mix) makes the 20,000-user total **NOK 49,070–53,070 excluding VAT**, before any extra server/database capacity. Media traffic is not a guaranteed ceiling.
- If video delivery is USD 0.01/GB instead of the assumed Volume price, add NOK **446 / 2,231 / 8,924**, respectively. At 20,000 users, that is about **NOK 33,500–37,500 total**, even at the original 100-view usage level.
- Doubling bytes delivered per video has the same video-bandwidth impact as doubling the video unit price, with possible additional storage costs. The bitrate/length caps in ADR-0012/0019 therefore matter.
- [Apple Developer](https://developer.apple.com/programs/enroll/): USD 99/year, about NOK 920 at the reference rate, with actual regional checkout price possibly different. Charge the whole annual invoice to launch cash needs if not already paid, rather than assuming a monthly installment.
- [Google Play Console](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/6112435?hl=en): USD 25 one-time registration, about NOK 232. Domain registration/renewal is additional at the actual registrar invoice. Do not silently count already-paid enrollment fees twice.
- Excludes development/AI subscriptions, salaries or purchased support/moderation, legal/accounting/company administration, marketing, payment-processing/store commissions if relevant to a future payment flow, premium vendor support, and the rest of the year's hosting/retained-media costs. These are not assumed to be free.
- No actual production traffic, provider invoices or load-test measurements were available. The quote and usage assumptions should be revisited before the 2027 season.
